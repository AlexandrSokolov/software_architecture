## Saga

How a business process that spans several services stays correct without one shared transaction.

<details><summary>Show topics</summary>

- Why one DB transaction can't span services; 2PC (XA) and why teams avoid it.
- Saga: a chain of local transactions, one per service.
- Compensation — undo a done step with a new action, not a rollback; what can't be undone (email sent, money paid out).
- Step order: steps that can still be undone, the point of no return (pivot step), steps retried until they succeed.
- No isolation: other requests see the half-done process; fixes such as a `PENDING` status that works as a lock.
- Every step and compensation must be safe to repeat — messages arrive twice.
