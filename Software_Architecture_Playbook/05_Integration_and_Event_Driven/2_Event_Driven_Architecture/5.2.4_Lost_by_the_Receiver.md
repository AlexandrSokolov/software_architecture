### Receiver

- You confirm the message on receipt, then crash before doing the work. What is lost?
- You confirm, then hand the work to an in-memory thread pool. What is the risk?
- You answer 200 to a webhook first, then do the work. What does that risk?
- Auto-commit is on. When does it lose messages, and when does it not?
- Ten threads handle one partition. Message 7 failed, 8–10 are done. Which position do you save?
- The handler catches an exception, logs it, and confirms the message. What did you just do?
- A message failed N times and is thrown away. Where must it go instead, and who looks at it?
- Spring Kafka: the retries for a record are used up. What happens to it by default? (verify)
- A consumer group has no saved position, because it is new or its position expired. Where does it start reading?
